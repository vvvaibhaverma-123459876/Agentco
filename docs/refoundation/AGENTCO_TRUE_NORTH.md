# Agentco True North

Status: **REAL** as product-positioning policy; **PARTIAL** as implementation.

Agentco's present-day promise:

> An evidence-governed control plane for autonomous systems: if an agent acts through Agentco, you can inspect the policy that governed it, the evidence it relied on, the calibration record behind its confidence, the attested environment it ran in, and the exact conditions under which it was permitted to affect the world.

The moat thesis is the combination of calibrated trust, external truth resolution, machine-enforced constitutions, and externally-verifiable provenance.

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. **Only reality promotes.** Simulation and fixtures may generate ideas, hypotheses, and candidate policies. They may never directly promote a claim to reality-validated truth. Promotion requires external evidence.
2. **Believe slowly.** Ingested information is never automatically true. All learned content carries an evidence status and earns trust through resolution.
3. **Authority is earned, not asserted.** No agent, source, model, dataset, or claim gets high authority by assertion. Authority comes from calibration, verified predictions, reliable history, reproducible experiments, external resolution, and longitudinal performance.
4. **Independence is mandatory.** A claim may not be resolved against the same source (or a non-independent derivative of the source) it came from. Circular verification must be mechanically impossible, not merely discouraged.
5. **Provenance is preserved.** Memory and trusted knowledge always retain provenance. No trusted memory without a provenance pointer.
6. **Evolution is governed.** Any meaningful self-modification passes pre-registration, sandboxing, evaluation, safety checks, rollback planning, and required approval, with audit logging.
7. **Protected surfaces are protected.** Self-generated code may not alter resolver truth rules, firewall promotion rules, audit-log integrity, signing logic, benchmark ground truth, frozen eval datasets, or security policy without an explicit high-risk governance process.
8. **Actions are first-class.** Claims are only one class of attestable object. Consequential actions, policy decisions, evidence extractions, and human overrides are also first-class attestable records with provenance.
9. **Validation distinguishes evidence quality.** Every validation result distinguishes REAL, EXTERNAL-VALIDATED, FIXTURE, simulated, and unresolved. Self-graded fixtures are never the top-line evidence for a calibration claim.
10. **Docs never outrun code.** The repo may not claim what its tests cannot prove. Every major layer has tests; every self-claim links to proof.
