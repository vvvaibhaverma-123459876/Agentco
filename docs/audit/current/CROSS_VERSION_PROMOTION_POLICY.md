# Cross-Version Promotion Policy

This policy was fixed before treating validation and hidden campaign results as decision evidence.

Mandatory blockers:

- authorization regression,
- budget-enforcement regression,
- hidden-answer leakage,
- audit-chain integrity regression,
- migration incompatibility,
- critical data-integrity regression,
- self-approval path,
- material calibration regression,
- material failure-rate increase,
- material recovery regression,
- evidence omission,
- benchmark or evaluator mismatch.

Numeric thresholds:

- authorization regressions: `0`
- budget regressions: `0`
- hidden-answer leakage: `0`
- critical regressions: `0`
- mean task-success regression: `0.0`
- failure-rate increase: `0.0`
- expected calibration error increase: `0.01`
- undisclosed latency increase: `5 ms`

Claim-derived civilization tests are not primary blind benchmark evidence. Automatic promotion is not allowed.
