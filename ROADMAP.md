# AgentCo Roadmap

This roadmap preserves the larger ambition without claiming it is launch-ready.

Current implementation status is tracked in [BUILD_LEDGER.yaml](BUILD_LEDGER.yaml) and summarized in [docs/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_REALITY.md](docs/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_REALITY.md). As of 2026-06-29, AgentCo is local-native runnable with `18/67` ledger items verified, not production certified.

## Current Product

AgentCo's current verified wedge is evidence-grounded learning: registered evidence, grounded supported claims, pre-registered predictions, `resolution_service`-only resolution, persistent trust scoring, prediction-lesson memory promotion, and immutable event/audit records.

## Future: Full Company Layer

Future work may turn the calibrated-agent substrate into a fuller operating company: departments, business workflows, customer operations, finance, legal, sales, marketing, and human governance workflows. These should ship only when they are executable product surfaces, not architecture claims.

## Future: Civilization Layer

The broader civilization/society/economy/culture/law/education ambitions are explicitly future work. No launch claim should imply AgentCo currently implements societies, culture, law, economy, education, or a general civilization OS.

## Future: Production Security

Identity, authority chains, key lifecycle, and some production fail-closed guards exist. Full production Vault/KMS posture, real service smoke, and operational deployment proof remain roadmap.

## Future: Trustlessness

Proof-of-Calibration scores are independently recomputable today, and credentials can be signed. A federated reserve with multiple independent signers, public transparency logs, and broader third-party oracle markets is future work.
