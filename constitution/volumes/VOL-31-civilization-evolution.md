## 2. Purpose

Civilization Evolution is the highest layer: how AgentCo changes *itself* — its
constitution, architecture, institutions, capabilities, and governance — over decades,
while preserving constitutional continuity, auditability, and human oversight. It is the
capstone the whole constitution builds toward, and deliberately the last charter, because
it may only be designed once the layers beneath it are real.

**Scope (one paragraph).** Civilization DNA (a generative blueprint from which institutions,
policies, and architectures emerge); constitutional, architectural, institutional,
scientific, capability, model, and governance evolution; recursive self-improvement;
long-term memory and lineage; extinction and recovery planning; multi-civilization
experimentation; civilization forks and merges; civilization health metrics; frontier
detection; and continuous reconciliation between design, implementation, and observed
reality. Every one of these is subordinate to the one invariant that cannot evolve away:
**human root authority and the protected-invariant mechanism remain outside the system's
own amendment power** (V1-INV-008, V19-INV-009).

**This layer is not yet designed in detail.** It is a charter — the most aspirational one.
Nothing here exists today beyond its seeds: the ledgers as a lineage record, the kernel's
versioning (V2), structural evolution's validation substrate (V19), and self-inspection's
drift detection (V17). Recursive self-improvement, civilization forks/merges, and
multi-civilization experimentation are entirely unbuilt.

**Detailed design may begin when:** (a) Structural Evolution (V19) can actually evolve
structure safely with continuity preservation proven (V19-INV-009 enforced); (b)
Autonomous Evolution (V16) runs its six loops under governance; (c) the Self Model (V18)
and Self Inspection (V17) give the system a current, queryable picture of itself to evolve
*against*; and (d) — the hard gate — a demonstrated, tested guarantee that no
self-evolution can remove human root authority or a protected invariant. Until all four
hold, civilization-level self-evolution stays a charter and a design aspiration, and any
change to the civilization goes through the ordinary governed paths (V12/V19) under human
authority. This is the ceiling of the vision (V0) and, by design, the slowest gate to open.
